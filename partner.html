What we look for

Quality you can show us on paper.

FDA facility registration is a starting point, not proof of quality. We'll ask for the records behind it.

  • Dietary supplement cGMP (21 CFR Part 111) — batch records, SOPs, and recent audit or inspection results.
  • Domestic or international — FDA-registered facilities anywhere, with the documentation U.S. import rules require for foreign suppliers.
  • A certificate of analysis for every lot — with identity testing and the exact ingredient form stated.
  • Specifications in writing — fill weight, overage policy and stability data before we commit.
  • Direct access to your technical team — not only sales or a broker.
  • Realistic first-run minimums — we're starting small and growing with the right partner.

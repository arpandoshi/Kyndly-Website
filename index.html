What is KyndLyte?

Two ingredients in a plant-based capsule: sodium chloride and potassium citrate. No added flavors, colors or sweeteners — a tasteless, measured amount you can count, capsule by capsule.

  • 250 mg sodium per capsule
  • 65 mg potassium per capsule
  • USP-grade ingredients
  • HPMC plant-based capsule
  • Vegan & gluten-free

Our approach

Simple supplements, made carefully.

  • Measured amounts

    Every formula states the exact amount of each ingredient per capsule, printed plainly on the label.

  • Short ingredient lists

    If an ingredient doesn't need to be there, it isn't. We name the exact form we use — like potassium citrate, anhydrous.

  • Verified, lot by lot

    We work with cGMP-compliant manufacturing facilities and require a certificate of analysis, with identity testing, for every lot.

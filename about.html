About Kyndly

A small brand, kept simple on purpose.

Kyndly Health is a Texas-based dietary supplement brand. We formulate our own products and have them made in cGMP-compliant manufacturing facilities, then bring them to you under the Kyndly name.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Plain labels

    Exact amounts, exact ingredient forms, nothing hidden in a "proprietary blend".

  2. 02

    Verified ingredients

    A certificate of analysis with identity testing for every lot, checked against our written specification.

  3. 03

    Honest claims

    We say what's in the bottle and what the law allows a supplement to say. Nothing more.

  4. 04

    Certified, wherever they are

    We partner with domestic and international manufacturers that meet U.S. dietary supplement cGMP and hold the certifications to prove it.
